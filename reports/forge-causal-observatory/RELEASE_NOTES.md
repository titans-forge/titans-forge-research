# Release Notes

## v0.1.0 - October 14, 2026

Initial saved-evidence verification release of Forge Causal Observatory.

- Includes all three frozen paired trials: seeds 211, 223 and 227.
- Agent causal passes: 0/3; deterministic cheap baseline: 1/3.
- Preserves the original gates, budgets, outcomes and scientific limitations.
- Provides a standard-library-only Python verifier and 10 test methods
  covering 27 rehashed semantic corruption cases.
- Publishes synthetic evidence without the private runtime, checkpoints,
  credentials, operational ledgers or machine-specific metadata.
- Applies the owner-approved Forge Research Hosting and Distribution
  License 1.0 to this release only; other reports retain their existing terms.

This release verifies saved evidence, not training or inference execution
authenticity. It does not replay interventions or establish a general
agent advantage. No experiments were rerun to prepare the public release.
