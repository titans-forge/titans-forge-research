# The Brain's Night Shift

## A Sleep-Mitophagy Hypothesis Relevant to Cognitive Aging

**v6 | October 15, 2026 | Research hypothesis, not established dementia prevention**

[Read the report](report.md) | [Source ledger](../SOURCES.md) | [Correction history](../CORRECTIONS.md) | [Medical research index](../README.md)

The question is whether disrupted sleep and impaired mitochondrial quality control jointly identify a vulnerability that either domain alone misses. The combined mechanism is unproven. Animal cognition, human peripheral biomarkers and acute CSF/plasma concentration changes must not be mistaken for evidence that a combined intervention delays dementia.
