## Withdrawn Summaries

The earlier pipeline used search/query-derived compound labels and seeded evidence tiers. Different identifiers could describe the same DOI, while endpoint rows were not independent trials. Therefore its ranked scores, human/preclinical/null row totals, strongest-lane assertions and simulation-based detection probabilities are not retained as evidence. Removing them is not evidence of no effect; it removes unsupported precision.

The July 7, 2026 registry snapshot is historical context only. A missing direct stack trial in that snapshot is not proof that no such trial exists today. Recruitment labels are not refreshed here. Registrations are not outcome evidence and must not be counted again when a linked results paper is already present.

## The Brain's Night Shift v6

The title remains a research metaphor. The combined sleep-mitophagy mechanism is explicitly a hypothesis, not a demonstrated interaction. The previous repair phenotype score, numerical go/no-go cutoffs, citation-completeness score and evidence-upgraded flag are not validated metrics and have been removed. Proposed measurement domains are kept separate from held-out cognitive outcomes. Publication does not confer clinical validation.

Absolute local paths and stale Word/PDF/figure links were removed. v5 and older local drafts are not part of this release. This record documents the substantive corrections without implying that earlier model-generated audit statements constitute independent peer review.

## Remaining Review Work

Full-text appraisal of allocation, analysis populations, missingness, prespecification, multiplicity, selective reporting, conflicts and data/code availability is incomplete. No exhaustive correction/retraction screen, new systematic search, pooled effect estimate or independent laboratory validation is claimed. These limitations apply even where a bibliographic identifier resolves correctly.
