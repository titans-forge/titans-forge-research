# Muscle Endurance, Fatigue and Recovery

**Corrected research edition | October 15, 2026 | Research only**

## Summary

The selected UA literature contains human exercise signals, but endpoint and population distinctions matter. Strength, local muscle endurance, walking, ATP production, competitive performance and subjective fatigue are not equivalent. These studies do not test the proposed four-component combination or establish treatment of a fatigue syndrome.

| Study | Main interpretation | Important boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Middle-aged UA trial, four months [S18](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35584623/) | Selected strength and secondary measures favored UA. | Primary peak power did not significantly improve. |
| Older-adult UA trial, 66 participants [S19](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35050355/) | Some secondary endurance and plasma measures improved. | Primary six-minute walk and maximal ATP-production comparisons were not significant. |
| Resistance-trained men, 20 participants [S00](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39487653/) | Some isometric/repetition measures improved. | Between-group one-repetition maximum comparisons were not significant; inflammatory measures were mixed. |
| Trained male runners, 42 randomized with smaller performance/biopsy subsets [S04](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40839339/) | Some exertion, damage-marker and proteomic findings were reported. | No additional running-performance benefit established; no mitochondrial-function change. |
| CoQ10 exercise review [S17](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35565783/) | Context for heterogeneous exercise findings. | Review, not another randomized participant sample or proof of a pooled benefit. |

## What Not to Infer

A significant secondary measurement does not turn a nonsignificant primary endpoint into trial success. A nonsignificant result also does not prove equivalence or no possible effect; effect estimates and uncertainty need full-text review. Within-group gains during training can occur without a treatment-specific benefit.

Recovery markers such as creatine kinase and subjective exertion are not substitutes for improved performance, clinical recovery or fewer injuries. Direct UA exposure compared with pomegranate juice is a bioavailability comparison, not a demonstration that supplementation improves physical function. [S23](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34117375/)

## An Informative Follow-up

Choose the intended population before selecting the endpoint. For example, older adults with objectively impaired function and trained competitors have different baseline constraints and relevant outcomes. Prespecify one primary performance/function comparison, training exposure, dietary control, adherence, adverse-event collection and handling of missing results. Treat molecular and proteomic measures as supportive, with a stated multiplicity policy.

Replication of a targeted, meaningful primary effect is more informative than adding many exploratory biomarkers. Only after such replication would a component-controlled combination test address whether other ingredients add value.

## Boundary

This is a selected record/abstract review, not a full-text systematic appraisal, meta-analysis or prescribing guide. No universal endurance effect, fatigue treatment, combination safety or synergy claim is made.
