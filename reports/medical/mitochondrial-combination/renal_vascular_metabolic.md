# Renal, Vascular and Metabolic Research Hypotheses

**Corrected research edition | October 15, 2026 | Research only**

## The Crucial Identity Correction

The chronic kidney disease pilot used **MitoQ**, not PQQ. MitoQ is also distinct from ordinary CoQ10/ubiquinol. In eighteen participants over four weeks, the abstract reports a flow-mediated dilation signal and no exercise-capacity benefit; the microvascular comparison was borderline at P=.053. It does not establish improved kidney function, renal disease modification, PQQ efficacy or efficacy of the four-component proposal. [S01](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37560769/)

## Adjacent Evidence, Not a Kidney Treatment Case

- The CoQ10 cardiovascular article is a narrative review and cannot be counted as a PQQ trial. The xenobiotic-metabolism overview is also CoQ10 review-level context. [S02](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32331285/), [S08](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40565253/)
- A UA trial in low producers with relatively poor endothelial function explored flow-mediated dilation and microbiome responses. Baseline phenotype, subgroup selection and uncorrected microbiome testing constrain interpretation. This is not a kidney clinical-outcome study. [S12](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36687672/)
- UA cardiac work combines preclinical functional results with human circulating ceramide changes. Human biomarkers do not establish fewer cardiac events, better renal function or disease reversal. [S32](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40034121/)
- The small PQQ lipid trial found no triglyceride change, marginal overall LDL findings and a stronger selected-subgroup result. It does not establish cardiovascular or renal protection. [S21](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26226960/)

## Next Test Worth Designing

Define whether the question is endothelial physiology, kidney function or patient function. A surrogate vascular endpoint cannot silently replace a renal outcome. Document baseline renal impairment, concurrent therapies, exposure, safety monitoring and measurement reproducibility. Any future trial in a medically vulnerable population needs independent clinical, ethical and statistical oversight before recruitment.

A properly designed mechanistic study could ask whether an exposure-linked endothelial change replicates, with a prespecified comparator and confidence intervals. It would still not establish disease modification or justify substituting one chemically distinct quinone for another.

## Boundary

The selected record/abstract audit supports a corrected evidence map, not a treatment recommendation or current trial-registry census. Full-text bias, interaction and safety appraisal remains incomplete. No renal benefit, combination safety or synergy is established.
