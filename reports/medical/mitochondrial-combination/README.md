# Mitochondrial Combination Research

**Six corrected reports | October 15, 2026 | Research only**

The proposed combination is CoQ10/ubiquinol, pyrroloquinoline quinone (PQQ), urolithin A (UA) and docosahexaenoic acid (DHA). These compounds are not interchangeable, and separate component findings do not establish an effective or synergistic four-part intervention. MitoQ is a distinct adjacent compound, not another name for PQQ or ordinary CoQ10.
