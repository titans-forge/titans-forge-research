# Oxidative Stress and Inflammatory Resilience

**Corrected research edition | October 15, 2026 | Research only**

## Research Question

Can an intervention improve a prespecified functional outcome while changing a biologically relevant stress or inflammatory measure? A change in a convenient biomarker alone is not proof of greater resilience, reduced disease burden or effective combination therapy.

## What the Selected Studies Show

- In the ubiquinol burn-patient trial, circulating and peripheral-cell CoQ10 exposure increased, but reported inflammatory markers, circulating mitochondrial DNA and SOFA did not significantly improve. It is a mixed endpoint result, not a wholly negative uptake experiment or demonstrated recovery benefit. [S05](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35888737/)
- The middle-aged UA trial reported selected biomarker/strength signals, but its primary peak-power endpoint was not significantly improved. The older-adult trial also had nonsignificant primary walk and ATP-production comparisons despite secondary endurance and plasma signals. [S18](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35584623/), [S19](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35050355/)
- In twenty resistance-trained men, UA findings varied by measure. Between-group isometric/repetition outcomes improved while one-repetition maximum did not; within-group CRP increased even though the between-group CRP comparison favored UA. Baseline and between-group contrasts must not be conflated. [S00](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39487653/)
- Runner recovery-marker/proteomic findings did not establish faster running or improved mitochondrial function. [S04](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40839339/)
- A PQQ cell experiment supports CREB/PGC-1alpha pathway dependence. Knockdown blocking the response is not a negative clinical trial and does not validate a human inflammatory endpoint. [S34](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19861415/)

## A More Informative Test

Select one clinically interpretable functional endpoint before choosing a biomarker panel. Prespecify assay reliability, timing, batch handling, missing-data rules and multiplicity control. Training load, infection, adiposity, medication changes and baseline inflammation are plausible competing explanations to measure rather than retrospectively explain away.

Exposure, pathway markers, mitochondrial function and clinical function should be analyzed as different levels. A peripheral pathway change is not automatically an organ-level benefit, and a static marker is not necessarily a measure of mitophagy flux.

The hypothesis should lose support if functional outcomes do not improve under adequate exposure and reliable measurement, or if apparently favorable effects depend entirely on unplanned analyses. Unexpected or adverse changes must remain in the record.

## Boundary

This is a selected bibliographic/abstract synthesis, not a systematic review or a completed full-text medical-rubric appraisal. No pooled anti-inflammatory effect, resilience score, validated surrogate, combination safety or four-component synergy is established. No treatment or dosing recommendation follows.
