# Neurocognitive and Neurovascular Research Hypotheses

**Corrected research edition | October 15, 2026 | Research only**

## Keep Four Questions Separate

An intervention can enter the circulation, alter a CSF lipid, change a cognitive test or affect disease incidence. Those are different claims. This selection does not establish dementia prevention or treatment by the proposed four-component combination.

## Human Findings and Their Limits

**PQQ alone:** A twelve-week product-specific trial randomized 64 volunteers reporting forgetfulness; 58 completed. The abstract reports cognitive-battery improvements. This is a signal requiring full-text scrutiny of prespecification, missingness, multiple comparisons and clinical importance, not a replicated dementia-prevention finding. [S20](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34415830/)

**Hydrogen plus PQQ:** The six-week MCI study randomized 34 people to a combined product. The primary BDNF group-by-time result was not significant (P=.14); a secondary orientation result was reported at P=.03. Neither within-group improvement nor the secondary endpoint establishes the prespecified primary result or isolates PQQ's contribution. [S22](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38908296/)

**DHA:** A small human CSF lipid pilot, accompanied by mouse work, studied lipidomic and APOE-related differences. It addresses exposure-related biology, not demonstrated cognitive benefit. MFSD2A transport discussions provide mechanism, not evidence that an ordinary oral formulation achieves a therapeutic brain effect. [S13](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36958720/), [S15](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26747400/), [S16](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41469845/)

**UA:** Cognitive and lysosomal findings in three Alzheimer mouse models are preclinical. Human muscle, plasma and immune studies cannot be relabeled as human CNS mitophagy engagement or dementia efficacy. [S30](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38753870/), [S19](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35050355/), [S03](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41174221/)

**MitoQ:** The CKD vascular pilot is not a PQQ trial or a cognitive-outcome trial. A peripheral endothelial signal does not by itself demonstrate cerebral perfusion or neurovascular benefit. [S01](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37560769/)

## Proposed Next Research Step

First resolve whether a chosen candidate has reproducible exposure and a defensible human CNS-relevant target-engagement measure. A later cognitive study needs a prespecified primary endpoint, an appropriate comparator, practice-effect controls, adequate follow-up and independent replication. Neurovascular and cognitive outcomes must not be substituted for one another after results are known.

For a combination claim, component-controlled or factorial evidence is necessary. A four-component arm versus placebo alone cannot identify which ingredient mattered or establish an interaction. Sleep, apnea, vascular risk, education, medication and baseline disease burden are important alternative explanations.

## Boundary

Bibliographic records and abstracts were checked; full methods, supplements, registration and conflict-of-interest appraisal remain incomplete. No validated CNS biomarker bridge, dementia-delay effect, combination efficacy or patient dosing guidance is provided.
